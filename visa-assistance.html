Documentation & Legal

Visa Assistance.

Expert documentation guidance and application support. We help you present the strongest possible case to embassies and immigration authorities.

Important Disclaimer: Visa approval is the sole discretion of the respective embassy or immigration authority. SKATS Travels & Tours provides documentation preparation and guidance only. We cannot and do not guarantee visa approvals.

Visa Types We Assist With

Our specialists cover a wide range of visa categories for destinations worldwide.

Tourist / Visitor Visa

Short-term leisure travel documentation for holidaymakers and family visits across major global destinations.

Business Visa

Documentation support for executive visits, trade negotiations, conferences, and professional engagements.

Student / Study Visa

Comprehensive preparation for university-linked visa applications across the UK, Canada, US, and Europe.

Work Permit

Assistance with employer-sponsored work permits, documentation audits, and co-ordination with sponsors.

Medical Visa

Streamlined preparation for medical treatment visits, including hospital invitation letter coordination.

Family Reunion

Documentation support for family unification and dependent visa applications at major embassies.

How It Works

Our Visa Process

01

Initial Consultation

A visa specialist reviews your travel intent, destination requirements, and current document status.

02

Document Checklist

We provide a precise, destination-specific checklist of required documents based on the embassy's current guidelines.

03

Document Audit & Review

Our specialists meticulously audit each document for accuracy, completeness, and embassy compliance.

04

Application Filing

We coordinate form completion, appointment scheduling, and submission to the relevant embassy or visa center.

05

Decision & Follow-up

We track your application status and assist with any additional embassy queries or supplemental document requests.
